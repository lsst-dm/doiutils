### New features

- Added the ``--update-authors``/``--no-update-authors`` options to ``update-paper-info``.
  Authors are now retained by default so that only the relationships in the DOI record are updated, leaving the author affiliations as they were when the paper was published.
  Use ``--update-authors`` to refresh the authors from the author database.
  If neither option is given a warning is issued when the author list has changed for a reason other than affiliation, since a change in author membership or ordering is worth knowing about even when affiliation drift is being ignored.

### Bug fixes

- YAML configurations are no longer written with trailing whitespace.
  A long plain scalar, such as a document title, is folded at the output width and the space replaced by the line break was retained at the end of the line.
- ``find-internal-citations`` no longer adds a duplicate ``IsCitedBy`` entry on every invocation.
  It now also sorts each relationship and removes any repeated DOIs, and records the inverse relationship for configurations that have no ``relationships`` section.
